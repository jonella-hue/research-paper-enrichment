A test of searching the full written content of papers, not just titles and summaries. 25 candidates from the Tech Pilot sheet (all 14 Strong fits plus 11 Possible from CS, EE and Physics), up to 8 free papers each. Full text comes from arXiv and open-access PDFs found through OpenAlex. Reference lists are left out so a search does not match other people's paper titles.

The same data is stored in a searchable database file (papers_fulltext.db) to test how this would work at full scale.
